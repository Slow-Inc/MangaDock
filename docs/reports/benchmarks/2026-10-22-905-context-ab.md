# Cross-page context (#159) A/B: mixed — helps coherence sometimes, carries errors other times; names stay inconsistent

- **Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Lever:** `MIT_CONTEXT_PAGES` (rolling cross-page dialogue context, default **0 = off**) ·
  **Path:** `translate_batch_pipelined` (real pages, GPU1, replay-free).
- **Set:** 3 two-page chapter pairs (Boku p0/p1, Horimiya p0/p1, Tsuki p0/p1), arms `MIT_CONTEXT_PAGES=0` vs `2`.
  Region counts identical across arms (17/13, 30/28, 16/21).

## Targeted judge (two pages at once: name spellings, page-2 coherence, meaning errors)

| series | arm | page-2 coherence | mistranslations | name spellings |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Boku | off | 1 | 1 (`IVE GOT ALLERGIES` -> invented `ของมั้น`) | — |
| Boku | **on** | **2** | **0** | — |
| Tsuki | off | 2 | 1 (`月` -> `เดือน` month instead of moon) | 2 spellings (สึกุโยมิ / ทสึคุโยมิ) |
| Tsuki | **on** | 2 | **4** (p2 invented content; one garbled Thai `ฉันทองการจะเจา`) | 2 spellings (ทสึคุโยมิ / ทสึคโยมิ) |
| Horimiya | off/on | judge parse failed both arms | — | — |

## Reading

- **Context is not a free win.** It clearly helped one pair (Boku: coherence 1->2, stomped an invented word) and
  clearly hurt another (Tsuki page 2: four meaning errors, one garbled line — the injected prior dialogue can
  drag the model off the current page). Net: no justified default change, so `MIT_CONTEXT_PAGES` stays **0**.
- **It does not fix name spellings**: TSUKUYOMI still came out with two different Thai spellings across two pages in
  *both* arms (and a third spelling overall: สึกุโยมิ / ทสึคุโยมิ / ทสึคโยมิ). That is the real cross-page defect —
  a focused name-glossary context (names only, not the whole prior dialogue) is the next experiment (#980).

## Cost

Wall time unchanged within noise (Boku 16.3 s off vs 15.0 s on; Horimiya 21.8 vs 18.8; Tsuki 17.6 vs 17.2) — the
extra prompt tokens are negligible; the risk is quality, not latency.

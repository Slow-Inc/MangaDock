# `detector.detection_size` 3200 — recovers missed lines, but churns every page's reads (opt-in)

- **Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Knob:** `detector.detection_size` (default 2560; Backend env `MIT_DETECTION_SIZE`).
- **Motivation:** #883's leftover English credits are lines DBNet never detects. `text_threshold` 0.3 recovered
  them but rewrote the whole corpus (rejected); a higher detection resolution recovers them without touching
  thresholds.

## Measurements

| | 2560 (default) | 3200 |
|---|---|---|
| Chainsaw p0 textlines | 15 | **19** (the `NOTICE: You can read this chapter…` paragraph appears) |
| warm detection per page (4-page warm set) | ~473 ms | **~584 ms** (+23 %, ~+110 ms) |
| corpus pages whose region source texts change | — | **24-37 / 39** (mostly tiny re-reads from shifted boxes) |
| genuinely recovered text | — | Chainsaw credit paragraph, Mieruko fortune/recipe lines, a few dialogue lines |
| junk also picked up | — | kana/CJK art glyphs (`ん`, `刀`, `ベ`, `才`, `・乙女ゲ…`) — the #886 glyph guard drops the large ones |

## Decision — opt-in, default stays 2560

This is a tradeoff, not a free win: +110 ms/page and read churn on most of the corpus for a handful of
recovered lines (and some new art-glyph candidates). Per the quality-loop rule, a tradeoff stays non-default.
`MIT_DETECTION_SIZE=3200` (Backend env) or `--set detector.detection_size=3200` (Doctor) turns it on for
maximum-recall use.

## Also learned

Detection is **not bit-deterministic** (cuDNN kernels in DBNet): two runs of the same config differ by a few
textlines (`1433` vs `1428` across the corpus) and by small OCR re-reads. Future detection A/Bs must compare
sets with a duplicate-tolerance, not counts.

# #1055 — forced-failure measurement of segment-only retry: saves ~1.2-1.6 s per glitch, stays opt-in (quality risk real)

- **Date:** 2026-10-23 · harness: `tools/forced_glitch_retry_bench.py` (corrupts the first translation so a guard
  fires, then measures the retry path; real gateway, same page, alternated arms).

## Latency (warm reps, one forced glitch per page)

| arm | wall |
|---|---|
| default whole-page retry | 6.8 s / 16.2 s (cold-ish) |
| `MIT_SEGMENT_RETRY=1` | 5.2 s / 5.6 s |

→ **~1.2-1.6 s (20-25 %)** off a glitched page on warm runs. With the measured guard rate (~1 page in 6) that is
~0.2-0.3 s of average per-page latency.

## Quality (same forced line, `EH.. YOURE JUST 60NNA SIT WITH ME?`)

| rep | whole-page retry | segment-only retry |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | `เฮ้... เธอแค่ 60 นนะ นั่งกับฉันได้ไหม?` | `เฮ้... เธอแค่ 60 นน. นั่งกับฉันได้ไหม?` |
| 1 | `เอ๋... เธอแค่อยากนั่งด้วยกันเฉยๆ น่ะเหรอ` | `เฮ้... เธอแค่อยากนั่งด้วยกันกับฉันงั้นเหรอ` |

Both acceptable here, but earlier runs of the same harness produced **garbled or truncated** retried lines with
segment-only retry (e.g. `เธอแค่ 60 นน. ...`, a line cut at `เพราะฉันอายุ`), which is exactly the context-loss risk
predicted. The whole-page retry keeps the page for the model and is the default.

## Decision

- **Opt-in stays:** latency gain is real but only on glitched pages; the quality risk (line isolated from the page)
  is observable even in a small sample, so it is not a free win.
- Live/targeted comparisons on natural guard triggers remain on #1055; this harness makes them reproducible.
## Follow-up: a neighbour-context note on the isolated retry (tried, reverted)

Tried to recover the lost quality by appending the **already-translated previous line** to each retried segment:
`[context: the previous line on this page reads "…" — keep the tone and speaker consistent with it.]`

| rep | whole-page retry | segment retry + context note |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | `เอ่อ... เธอแค่จะมานั่งกับฉันงั้นเหรอ?` | `เฮ้... เธอแค่ 60 นน. นั่งกับฉันได้รึเปล่` (still garbled) |
| 1 | `เอ่อ.. เธอแค่อยากนั่งกับฉันเฉยๆ หรอ?` | `เฮ้.. เธอแค่อยากนั่งด้วยกันเฉยๆ หรอ?` |

The hint did not reproducibly restore quality (1 of 2 still garbled) while adding prompt tokens, so it was
**reverted** rather than shipped — the simple subset stays behind `MIT_SEGMENT_RETRY=1` and the default
whole-page retry is unchanged.

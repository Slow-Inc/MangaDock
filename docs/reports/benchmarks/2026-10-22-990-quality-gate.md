## Why this matters for the loop

The #902 null calibration showed a same-model judge cannot resolve small differences; this gate is the
complementary *hard* signal: it is deterministic, cheap, and re-runnable per chapter. Future rounds can run it
after every replay change and treat a new violation as a regression, without a judge.

## Companion negative results (same round)

Broader deterministic scans over the stored corpus came back clean, which is why no new guard was added:

- punctuation drops: 9 raw hits, all false positives (`…` -> `...`, `?!` -> `⁈`),
- Thai integrity (leading combining marks, doubled marks, control chars) and unexpected Latin: **0 / 11,633**
  stored src/dst pairs.
## HEAD baseline + one refinement (round 1)

Ran the gate over **every HEAD live output** (19 files / 237 regions, EN and JP paths):

| result | detail |
|---|---|
| **1 flag** | `en_Horimiya_c10_p0` #11: source `Mornin?` -> `อ้าว...` — the `?` is absent |
| triage | **benign style variation**, not a defect: `อ้าว...` is a Thai interjection with a trailing
| | ellipsis; the question is a greeting, not a true question. Recorded rather than whitelisted. |

One refinement came out of it: a **final Thai question particle** (เหรอ/หรอ/หรือ/ไหม/มั้ย/รึ/เปล่า/ไหน…) now counts
as carrying the question when `?` is absent — Thai manga routinely drops the mark there. Unit-tested both ways.

Gate unit tests: **7 passed**.
